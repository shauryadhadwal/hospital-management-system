# invoice

**Context:** billing

## Purpose

The bill for a visit. A visit has at most one invoice. A visit with no charges, or a booking that was cancelled or marked no-show, has no invoice.

## Decisions

- **Kept as an entity.** Reason: the hospital has to bill, and `invoice_line`, `payment` and `insurance_claim` all refer to it.
- **Total, amount paid and balance are not stored.** They are worked out from the invoice lines, the payments and the paid insurance claims: balance = total − paid claims − payments. Reason: avoids stored figures that drift out of step with their source rows.
- **Status follows the normal flow: draft while lines are added, issued once the bill is final (before the claim and any payment), paid when the balance reaches zero.** `cancelled` is for exception flows, which are not designed yet.
- **`paid` is set by the application, in the same action as the payment that brings the balance to zero.** Reason: it is a stored copy of a derived fact (balance = 0), so it is never edited by hand and must not be set any other way. With paid claims in the formula, the last of the insurer's claim and the patient's payment to arrive is the one that closes the invoice.
- **An invoice is created only for a `completed` visit** (after discharge, with the bed stay closed), and issued once all its lines are in. A claim or payment can only be recorded on an `issued` invoice.
- **Invoice numbers have the form `INV-YYYY-NNNNNN`** (year, then a running six-digit number), generated by the application.

## Columns

| Column | Type | Key / constraints | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `id` | INT | PK | Auto-increment |
| `visit_id` | INT | FK -> visit, NOT NULL, UNIQUE | UNIQUE gives at most one invoice per visit |
| `invoice_number` | VARCHAR(30) | NOT NULL, UNIQUE | Printed bill number, e.g. INV-2026-000001 |
| `invoice_date` | DATE | NOT NULL |  |
| `status` | VARCHAR(20) | NOT NULL, DEFAULT 'draft' | draft, issued, paid, cancelled |
| `created_at` | DATETIME | NOT NULL, DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP | Set when the row is inserted |
| `updated_at` | DATETIME | NOT NULL, DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP | Set on insert and refreshed on every update |

Total, amount paid and balance are not stored; they come from `invoice_line`, `payment` and `insurance_claim` (status `paid`).

## Constraints

Sanity-level checks, in addition to the keys and NOT NULLs in Columns. A NULL passes a CHECK, so optional columns are only checked when filled in.

- `ck_invoice_status`: `status IN ('draft', 'issued', 'paid', 'cancelled')`
- `ck_invoice_number_not_blank`: `TRIM(invoice_number) <> ''`

## Relationships

- visit 1 -> 0..1 invoice
- invoice 1 -> 0..n invoice_line
- invoice 1 -> 0..n payment
- invoice 1 -> 0..n insurance_claim
